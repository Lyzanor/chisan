# Source Strategy

Use this reference only to choose and bound a source for a Level 1 area pass.
It does not define what qualifies for Chisan or what a source proves. Apply
`docs/EDITORIAL.md` and the target country's `AGENTS.md` for those decisions.

## Choose one source family

Prefer a current, no-cost public source that is inspectable, geographically
relevant and likely to expose identities not already represented. A practical
search order is:

1. producer associations, cooperatives or member collections;
2. agricultural, food-quality, fisheries or rural-development authorities;
3. protected-origin or certification operator directories;
4. sector bodies and trade associations;
5. routes, markets, fairs, awards or retailer supplier rosters;
6. a bounded set of targeted web queries.

This order prioritizes research efficiency only. It does not assign authority
to a source or satisfy an editorial claim. Record the applicable source ceiling
from the canonical country and editorial documents before retaining leads.

## Design a reproducible query

Tie queries to the target area, its public languages and official place names.
Useful shapes include:

- `<official municipality> <productive verb> <category>`;
- `<area or province> producer directory <category>`;
- `site:<authority-domain> <area> <operator or producer term>`;
- `<protected designation> producers`;
- `<sector association> members <area>`.

Declare the result window, municipalities, category or directory pages included
in the pass. Do not silently expand into another category, source family or
area.

## Stop conditions

End the pass when:

- the requested retained-lead limit is reached;
- the declared directory, result window or query set is exhausted;
- remaining results repeat catalog rows, open notes or tombstones;
- one reasonable public alternative cannot recover an unavailable source; or
- continuing would require paid access, bypassing access controls, a new scope
  or Level 2 admission research.

Report an unavailable source as an operational limitation. Apply the canonical
editorial policy to any inference about a producer.

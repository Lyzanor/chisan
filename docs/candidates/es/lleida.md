## RIAAC meat, bakery, preserve and other elaborators — identity reconciliation — 2026-09-04

- Discovery source: the [Indústries agroalimentàries de Catalunya](https://analisi.transparenciacatalunya.cat/Medi-Rural-Pesca/Ind-stries-agroaliment-ries-de-Catalunya/p28j-xe65) open dataset, which publishes the Registre d'Indústries Agràries i Alimentàries de Catalunya (RIAAC).
- Search date: 2026-09-04.
- Scope: the 69 remaining RIAAC main-activity classes that mean elaboration of food or drink for people — meat, prepared dishes, bakery and milling, preserves, dairy, nuts, fish, confectionery, drinks, condiments and the smaller classes — which return 3,221 rows for 1,745 distinct registrations. Handling, packing, bottling, storage, slaughter, cutting rooms, animal feed and non-food classes are excluded. De-duplicated by registration number, then against the area CSVs by name and municipality. 19 further registrations in this area sit in a municipality that has only one or two published rows of the same category, so each is held on one question: is it one of those rows under its trading name, or a different productive unit?
- Cutoff: the dataset as queried on 2026-09-04. This closes the RIAAC source: the olive-oil, honey, cheese and wine classes were taken in the two earlier batches.
- Source ceiling: see `data/csv/es/AGENTS.md` § Source ceilings. RIAAC supports the registered establishment, its activity class and the industrial address it publishes, never current activity, a public own offer or online sales. Its `municipi` cell carries the locality after the municipality.
- Known weakness of this batch: only 335 of the 1,745 registrations matched a published row, against 46% for the wine batch. These classes carry far more industrial and contract manufacturing than the artisanal classes did, and a plant that produces only for third-party labels fails the own-offer criterion. Expect a high rejection rate on review; the activity class in each row is the discriminator to start from.

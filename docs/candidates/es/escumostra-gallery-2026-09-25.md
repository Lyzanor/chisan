## Review conclusions

- Removing automatic drafts and the destructive replace/trim path reduced the tool while routing publication through the existing content editor. Regression checks cover old galleries, product references, translations, stale identity/content, tampered files and failed-apply cleanup.
- Large dimensions do not imply a useful photograph. The trial exposed stock images on an official site and award graphics competing with real production photos; known stock filenames and medal labels are now excluded before download.
- Explicit page context and permissions matter more than widening the crawl. Responsive/lazy sources, resolved redirects and page rotation address observed gaps without adding a browser crawler, event-specific service or recurring job.
- Website access and permissions remain manual exceptions. General galleries cannot be honestly marked complete for all 58 producers under the public-permission constraint. Keep the remaining queue on demand.

returns 242 operators of roughly 1,295 certified in total. The register's own CSV
export carries only name, address, locality, postcode, province, phone, fax,
email and website: **it publishes no product or activity column**, so the probable
category below is inferred from the operator name and must be confirmed. Vegetal
(784) and Animal (518) are raw production and are deferred; the *Apicultura*
filter returns nothing at all, which looks like a register defect rather than an
absence of beekeepers.

42 of the 242 are already represented, 27 by name, 2 only by their own web domain
and 13 on review. 15 operators seated outside Galicia are routed elsewhere and 13
were screened out. This note holds the Ourense share.
Organic certification is not evidence of elaboration, and the export's phone and

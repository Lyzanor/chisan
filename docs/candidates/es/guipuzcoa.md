- Discovery statements in the tables retain their original source limits. The remaining-work field records what still prevents admission; inaccessible or insufficient sources do not establish an exclusion.
- Resolved admissions, confirmed duplicates and closed exclusions have left this queue. Approved facts and closed decisions belong to the canonical CSVs and evidence ledgers.
- Editorial and source rules: `docs/EDITORIAL.md`, `docs/EVIDENCE_CONTRACT.md` and `data/csv/es/AGENTS.md`.
- The D.O.P. Euskal Sagardoa catalogue at `euskalsagardoa.eus/katalogoa/?lang=es` lists every inscribed cider house on one page, and each card carries a `data-project-cat` attribute of `alava`, `guipuzcoa` or `vizcaya`, so the page settles both membership and province in a single fetch. It publishes no address, no contact and no offer, so it never admits a row by itself — pair it with the cider house's own channel.
- Four of this queue's five cider houses are inscribed: Añota, Eguzkitza, Otatza and Txindurri iturri. **Iruretxiki is not**, so its hold cannot be closed through the council.
- Yurrita e Hijos is the published Conservas Yurrita row: `yurritagroup.com` publishes Ctra. a Ondarroa s/n, 20830 Mutriku, the same unit the catalog already carries under `tiendayurrita.com`. Group and shop domains of one company are worth checking against the published `web` column before treating a name as new.
- Two entries below are not Guipúzcoa productive units. Arizmendi Ardoak eta Gutiziak is listed at Sondika, a municipality of Bizkaia, and Pernod Ricard Bodegas answers from a Donostia commercial office for a national group whose wineries are elsewhere. Neither belongs in this area file.

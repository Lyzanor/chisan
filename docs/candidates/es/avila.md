# Candidate backlog — Ávila

- Target CSV: `data/csv/es/castilla-y-leon/avila.csv`
- Previous editorial review: 2026-09-20; it left 21 unresolved candidate entries.
- The pass reconciled candidate dossiers with the Spanish catalog, reviewed linked public sources where accessible, and resolved the artisan-register identities against the published rows by their industrial address. A dossier or registry check is not proof of current activity.
- Discovery statements in the tables retain their original source limits. The remaining-work field records what still prevents admission; inaccessible or insufficient sources do not establish an exclusion.
- Resolved admissions, confirmed duplicates and closed exclusions have left this queue. Approved facts and closed decisions belong to the canonical CSVs and evidence ledgers.
- Editorial and source rules: `docs/EDITORIAL.md`, `docs/EVIDENCE_CONTRACT.md` and `data/csv/es/AGENTS.md`.
- Two provincial sources informed this work. The Castilla y León artisan-food register publishes an accredited holder, activity class and industrial address; it helps identify a workshop but does not establish a public brand, current offer or current activity. Ávila Auténtica is a curated collective brand, not an exhaustive census, so absence from its member list is not evidence against a candidate. The collective shop can support an online-sales claim only when the producer and order path are attributable.

## Follow-up disposition review — 2026-09-23

The 21 open leads from the previous pass now resolve to six admissions, four closed exclusions, two candidates already represented by published producers, and nine holds.

- **Admitted:** Granja La Mesilla (Piedralaves); Mermelarte del Tiétar (La Adrada); Mermelicias de Gredos (El Hornillo); Hidromiel Decuria (Burgohondo); El Pomar de Gredos (Los Llanos de Tormes); and Miel Oro del Alberche (Navatalgordo). Their basic rows and decision sources are in the Ávila CSV and evidence ledger.
- **Verification labels:** Granja La Mesilla remains pending because a supplier profile is the production source, corroborated by the current municipal listing. Mermel-Arte del Tiétar remains pending because one product page says “La Iglesuela, Ávila” while its legal and artisan-register addresses place the productive unit in La Adrada. El Pomar de Gredos remains pending because the detailed provincial product record is from 2017 and the producer's listed domain currently returns 502; a sector feature still describes its cider and vinegar production in Hermosillo. Miel Oro del Alberche remains pending because the productive municipality is supported by the Tierra de Sabor profile and the artisan register; those sources differ on the street number, so no street address is published.
- **Hidromiel Decuria:** the initial Madrigal de las Altas Torres clue is corrected to Burgohondo. Its current site lists its meads; the 2024 Salón Gourmets exhibitor profile explicitly places its apiaries and factory in Burgohondo and describes mead made with its own honey; the Diputación names it among the producers selling at Músicos en la Naturaleza in 2026. The row remains pending because the productive-location claim relies on exhibitor-supplied catalogue information.
- **El Pomar de Gredos:** the artisan-register holder Esteban del Dedo Benito is linked to the public brand and production site by the same Santa Teresa industrial address. The provincial product page names its sidra natural, sidra manzanada and vinagre de sidra and locates its factory in Hermosillo; the municipality in the CSV is Los Llanos de Tormes. The listed official domain returns 502, so it is omitted from the row.
- **Miel Oro del Alberche:** the first-party site identifies the public honey brand with SAT Virgen de la Canaleja, whose own beekeeping yields and packages its own honey and pollen. Tierra de Sabor publishes both products under Oro del Alberche and places the productive address in Navatalgordo; artisan-register entry EAA-AV/0036 corroborates the SAT and honey activity there. The source street numbers differ, so the row omits the street address and remains pending. The site lists products but says it is being rebuilt, so online ordering remains unconfirmed.
- **Rejected as closed:** Industrias El Rey Niño, dissolved and extinguished in the 2025 BORME; Vidal Sánchez e Hijos / La Esmeralda, dissolved and extinguished in the 2023 BORME; Herranz de Pedro, whose Ávila Chocolates Coty factory ceased activity in 2017; and Luxtor, whose production assets were transferred to Irish Dairy Board España in 2014 and whose company was extinguished by merger in 2022.
- **Already represented:** EUGENIO ENCABO NIEVA is the legal holder of Quesos del Alberche Vailecus. The Tierra de Sabor profile places that dairy at Maestra Agustina 57, the industrial address in register entry EAA-AV/0366; this resolves the earlier address-based doubt. It remains one published Quesos del Alberche row. La Dehesa de Arévalo is a product line sold alongside González Perrino; its supplier profile shares the phone number published on the official Embutidos Perrino site and names the González Perrino range, so it is already represented by Embutidos Perrino.
- **Held:** Valviejo Brewery / Raíz Cuadrada has conflicting closure and activity signals: its official site says “CERRADO”, while the Princal Neolab profile still describes its beers and El Barraco plant. The available evidence does not establish permanent closure.
- The other 8 leads remain open on their previously recorded blockers. Lack of a suitable current source does not establish exclusion.

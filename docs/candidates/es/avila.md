- Discovery statements in the tables retain their original source limits. The remaining-work field records what still prevents admission; inaccessible or insufficient sources do not establish an exclusion.
- Resolved admissions, confirmed duplicates and closed exclusions have left this queue. Approved facts and closed decisions belong to the canonical CSVs and evidence ledgers.
- Editorial and source rules: `docs/EDITORIAL.md`, `docs/EVIDENCE_CONTRACT.md` and `data/csv/es/AGENTS.md`.
- Two provincial sources decided most of this pass and are worth reusing. The Castilla y León artisan-food register publishes a `domicilio_industrial` beside the holder and municipality: that elaboration address de-duplicates a bare personal name against a published trade name far better than the municipality alone, and it also places a workshop the trade name never publishes. Ávila Auténtica, the provincial collective brand, publishes a member page per producer with municipality, contact, site and product range at `avilaautentica.es/wp-json/wp/v2/asociados`, and a separate collective shop at `tienda.avilaautentica.es` whose cart is a first-party-equivalent order channel. Every candidate below was checked against both: none of them appears in either, which is itself the current state of the lead.

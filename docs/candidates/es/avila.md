# Candidate backlog — Ávila

- Target CSV: `data/csv/es/castilla-y-leon/avila.csv`
- Previous editorial review: 2026-09-20; it left 21 unresolved candidate entries.
- The pass reconciled candidate dossiers with the Spanish catalog, reviewed linked public sources where accessible, and resolved the artisan-register identities against the published rows by their industrial address. A dossier or registry check is not proof of current activity.
- Discovery statements in the tables retain their original source limits. The remaining-work field records what still prevents admission; inaccessible or insufficient sources do not establish an exclusion.
- Resolved admissions, confirmed duplicates and closed exclusions have left this queue. Approved facts and closed decisions belong to the canonical CSVs and evidence ledgers.
- Editorial and source rules: `docs/EDITORIAL.md`, `docs/EVIDENCE_CONTRACT.md` and `data/csv/es/AGENTS.md`.
- Two provincial sources informed this work. The Castilla y León artisan-food register publishes an accredited holder, activity class and industrial address; it helps identify a workshop but does not establish a public brand, current offer or current activity. Ávila Auténtica is a curated collective brand, not an exhaustive census, so absence from its member list is not evidence against a candidate. The collective shop can support an online-sales claim only when the producer and order path are attributable.

## Follow-up disposition review — 2026-09-23

The 21 open leads from the previous pass now resolve to three admissions, two closed exclusions, one candidate already represented by a published producer, and 15 holds.

- **Admitted:** Granja La Mesilla (Piedralaves); Mermelarte del Tiétar (La Adrada); Mermelicias de Gredos (El Hornillo). Their current basic rows and decision sources are in the Ávila CSV and evidence ledger.
- **Verification labels:** Granja La Mesilla remains pending because a supplier profile is the production source, corroborated by the current municipal listing. Mermel-Arte del Tiétar remains pending because one product page says “La Iglesuela, Ávila” while its legal and artisan-register addresses place the productive unit in La Adrada.
- **Rejected as closed:** Industrias El Rey Niño, dissolved and extinguished in the 2025 BORME; and Vidal Sánchez e Hijos / La Esmeralda, dissolved and extinguished in the 2023 BORME.
- **Already represented:** EUGENIO ENCABO NIEVA is the legal holder of Quesos del Alberche Vailecus. The Tierra de Sabor profile places that dairy at Maestra Agustina 57, the industrial address in register entry EAA-AV/0366; this resolves the earlier address-based doubt. It remains one published Quesos del Alberche row.
- **Held:** Valviejo Brewery / Raíz Cuadrada has conflicting closure and activity signals: its official site says “CERRADO”, while the Princal Neolab profile still describes its beers and El Barraco plant. The available evidence does not establish permanent closure.
- **Held:** Hidromiel Decuria's current site lists a mead range, but does not identify the productive municipality or provide an order path. The Fromago roster alone does not resolve those facts.
- The other 13 leads remain open on their previously recorded blockers. Lack of a suitable current source does not establish exclusion.

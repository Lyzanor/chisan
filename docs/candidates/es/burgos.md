- Editorial review: 2026-09-23; 40 unresolved candidate entries remain.
- The pass worked the Burgos Alimenta member directory, the Castilla y León artisan register and the queue's own first-party links. A directory or registry check is not proof of current activity.
- Discovery statements in the tables retain their original source limits. The remaining-work field records what still prevents admission; inaccessible or insufficient sources do not establish an exclusion.
- Resolved admissions, confirmed duplicates and closed exclusions have left this queue. Approved facts and closed decisions belong to the canonical CSVs and evidence ledgers.
- Editorial and source rules: `docs/EDITORIAL.md`, `docs/EVIDENCE_CONTRACT.md` and `data/csv/es/AGENTS.md`.
- Burgos Alimenta (`www.burgosalimenta.com`), the Diputación's provincial club, is the working directory for this area. Its listing is AJAX: `POST buscarempresas.php` with `Content-Type: application/json` and the body `{"categories":[]}` returns all 358 member cards in one response, each with `ficha-empresa.php?idEmpresa=<id>`, the trade name and the municipality. Each ficha then carries the productive address, postcode, phone, email and website. Ignore the comment threads on a ficha — they are lorem-ipsum placeholder text, not real comments.
- **Match the club's website column against the published `web` column, not the names.** That alone resolved seven members as already represented, including two entries of this queue whose names gave nothing away: `siestabrewing.es` showed Beer In Burgos is the published Siesta Brewing Co., and `lacuevadevadorrey.com` showed that *both* Matadero Frigorífico R. García Ribera Del Duero and Fábrica de Quesos Vadorrey are the published Quesos La Cueva de Vadorrey, one company at Ctra. Roa-Peñafiel s/n. The same column also identified Comercial Altoesgueva as Lechazo y Quesos del Vidal, Morcilla Cuevas as Cárnicas Cuevas Aranda, Huevos Himma as Granja Hima, Panaderías Cámara as El Horno de Burgos, Cerveza Victoria as Cerveza Dolina and Viñedos del Ternero as Hacienda El Ternero.
- The club admits members that are not producers, and its `M.G.` prefix marks a *marca de garantía* rather than a company: M.G. Manzana Reineta y Cereza de Caderechas, M.G. Lechuga de Medina, M.G. Alubia Roja de Ibeas, La Torta de Aranda (the regional bakers' federation) and Trufa de Burgos are collective marks and associations. Restaurants, hotels and shops are members too.
- Club membership does not place a row in this province, and a member's own site can contradict the club. Licores Lujo is listed at Nava Ordunte (Burgos) while its own site publishes only Avenida Cervantes 51, Pol. Ind. Denak, Basauri (Bizkaia); the Vizcaya pass of 2026-09-21 admitted it at Basauri, the only address the company itself publishes.
- Two club members that look like producers are not: Delicias Burgos is an online shop that attributes every item to its actual elaborator, and INCARSA publishes only matadero, sala de despiece, almacén frigorífico and SANDACH transformation services with no own product or brand. The Burgos potato firms in the club — Patatas Colina, Almacenes Arreba, Patatas Fernández Miguelón — publish storage, handling, packing and distribution, which is not elaboration; the same caution applies to the Patatas Adrian and Seyco Patatas rows below.
- In the Castilla y León artisan register the Burgos province code is `prov=BU`; query it with `refine.prov=BU`, which returns 40 entries with their `domicilio_industrial`.

## Cooperativas Agro-alimentarias directory pass — 2026-09-11

Scope: the federation's national directory, 2,171 cooperatives read from the
listing endpoint behind its search. Each record carries the name, address,
postcode, municipality, province, community, phone and the sectors it is
registered for; only 183 publish a website. 234 cooperatives register no food
sector at all — supplies and fuel, member shops, credit sections, animal feed,
fodder, tobacco, cotton, flowers or forestry — and are not producers through
those activities.

1,303 register an elaborating food sector (oil, wine, table olives, rice, milk,
sugar or honey); 147 of those are already represented. This batch takes the
**55 unmatched ones that publish a first-party website**, because the directory
itself cannot answer the criterion that decides these cases — whether a food
reaches the market under the cooperative's own identity — and a website is the
nearest thing to that evidence. The remaining 1,101 unmatched elaborating
cooperatives, and the raw-production sectors, are deferred.

Apply the collective admission test in `docs/EDITORIAL.md`; cooperative degree
does not decide eligibility. Source ceiling: `data/csv/es/AGENTS.md`.

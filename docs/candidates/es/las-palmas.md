# Candidate backlog — Las Palmas

- Target CSV: `data/csv/es/canarias/las-palmas.csv`
- Editorial review: 2026-09-20; 46 unresolved candidate entries remain.
- The pass worked the Cabildo de Gran Canaria producer directory and the queue's own first-party links. A directory check is not proof of current activity.
- Discovery statements in the tables retain their original source limits. The remaining-work field records what still prevents admission; inaccessible or insufficient sources do not establish an exclusion.
- Resolved admissions, confirmed duplicates and closed exclusions have left this queue. Approved facts and closed decisions belong to the canonical CSVs and evidence ledgers.
- Editorial and source rules: `docs/EDITORIAL.md`, `docs/EVIDENCE_CONTRACT.md` and `data/csv/es/AGENTS.md`.
- Gran Canaria Me Gusta is the island's food programme and runs on two hosts. `cabildo.grancanariamegusta.com/productos-y-productores/` is the Cabildo's producer directory: 13 paginated pages that curl reads as plain HTML, giving 236 entries with the trade name, the municipality, a delivery lead time, a phone and an email. `grancanariamegusta.com` is the collective PrestaShop; its brand routes (`/marcas`, `/fabricantes`) 404, and its sellers are instead at `/comercializador/<slug>`, listed from `/tiendas` — only ten of them. The directory's WordPress REST API is closed (401) and its `post-sitemap.xml` holds only blog posts, so the paginated listing is the way in.
- The Cabildo directory carries no address and no product detail, so it supports identity, municipality and contact but never admits a row alone. It is also not a producer roster: a large share of its entries are fruterías, pescaderías, carnicerías, floristerías and the island's municipal markets, most sharing a single market-manager email.
- Its municipality column can contradict the producer's own site, and the site wins. It files EcoArucas under Firgas while `ecoarucas.com` publishes Calle Marqueses de Arucas 20, 35400 Arucas; it files Granja Avícola La Pardilla under Ingenio against this queue's Telde; and it files Granja Avícola Teror under Teror against this queue's Las Palmas de Gran Canaria.
- **Read the legal notice before placing a Canary row.** Cafés Careca publishes a Las Palmas de Gran Canaria phone but its aviso legal gives the roasting plant and registered office at Pol. Ind. de Güímar, 38509 Valle de Güímar, Santa Cruz de Tenerife; the row belongs to that queue, not this one. An island-group brand with a commercial presence on Gran Canaria is not by itself a Las Palmas productive unit.

- Editorial and source rules: `docs/EDITORIAL.md`, `docs/EVIDENCE_CONTRACT.md` and `data/csv/es/AGENTS.md`.
- **Alimentos y Vinos de Castilla-La Mancha** (`foodandwineclm.com`, the Junta's agri-food export directory) is a lever for every Castilla-La Mancha queue. Its WordPress REST type is open: `wp-json/wp/v2/empresa?per_page=100&page=1..2` returns all 191 companies, but the REST content omits the location. Each ficha page carries a DESCRIPCIÓN, a PRODUCTOS list and an address under “ESTAMOS EN …”; read the province from the postcode prefix (02 Albacete, 13 Ciudad Real, 16 Cuenca, 19 Guadalajara, 45 Toledo), since some fichas write “(AB)” and others “(Albacete)”. It lists 26 Albacete companies. The fichas and the REST pages are cached in the repository's ignored `.tmp/foodandwineclm/`. It placed Polgri and Granja Ecológica Villada, which have no working site of their own, gave Goyval's street address, and confirmed Eurochoc's manufacturing.
- The D.O.P. Azafrán de La Mancha council publishes its registered packers at `doazafrandelamancha.com/es/productores-y-envasadores/envasadores-de-azafran-con-denominacion-de-origen.html`: eight in Albacete, and none of them is one of this queue's six saffron names. Saffron packers often buy stigmas rather than grow them, so each saffron lead still needs evidence of its own cultivation and toasting.
- Duplicates the names hid: Bodegas Almanseñas is the published Bodegas Venta la Vega at the same Ctra. de Alpera CM-3201 km 98,6; the direct-sale entry Bodegas César Velas… is the published César José Velasco Almodóvar; the RGSEAA entry SPANISH CHOCOLATE, S.L. is the Amalia Chocolate holder; and FRUTAS Y TURRONES, S.A. is Turrones San Andrés. Loose name matching produces many false pairs in this province (“San”, “Cruz”, “Vega”); match on the address or the domain.
- Several producer domains no longer exist: `caminodelacingla.com`, `alquitaramanchega.com` and `coqueya.com` return NXDOMAIN from public DNS, and `granjaecologicavillada.com` returns SERVFAIL. This was checked against Google's resolver, so it is not a local DNS fault. A lapsed domain is not evidence of closure; search engines still index their old pages.

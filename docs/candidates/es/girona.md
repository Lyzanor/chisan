Handling, packing, bottling, storage, slaughter, cutting rooms, animal feed and non-food classes are excluded. De-duplicated by registration number, then against the area CSVs by name and municipality. 62 further registrations in this area sit in a municipality that has only one or two published rows of the same category, so each is held on one question: is it one of those rows under its trading name, or a different productive unit?
- Cutoff: the dataset as queried on 2026-09-04. This closes the RIAAC source: the olive-oil, honey, cheese and wine classes were taken in the two earlier batches.
- Source ceiling: see `data/csv/es/AGENTS.md` § Source ceilings. RIAAC supports the registered establishment, its activity class and the industrial address it publishes, never current activity, a public own offer or online sales. Its `municipi` cell carries the locality after the municipality.
- Known weakness of this batch: only 335 of the 1,745 registrations matched a published row, against 46% for the wine batch. These classes carry far more industrial and contract manufacturing than the artisanal classes did, and a plant that produces only for third-party labels fails the own-offer criterion. Expect a high rejection rate on review; the activity class in each row is the discriminator to start from.

# Portugal discovery methods

Apply the [shared candidate workflow](../README.md) and the
[Portugal rules and source ceilings](../../../data/csv/pt/AGENTS.md). Record the
exact source, query, review date and cutoff in the relevant area note.

## Reusable sources

- [Produtos Tradicionais Portugueses](https://www.ptpt.pt/produtores) publishes
  traditional-producer profiles with declared address and contact.
- [ANICP](https://anicp.pt/associados/) and
  [Casa do Azeite](https://casadoazeite.pt/pt/associados) publish sector member
  directories.
- [Marca Açores](https://www.marcaacores.pt/promotores) can locate certified
  promoters by category and island.
- Regulatory councils, municipal chambers and local associations can lead from
  a DOP, IGP or regional speciality to individual producers.

Use business, tourism and collective-storefront directories for discovery only.
Follow them to the named productive unit before admission.

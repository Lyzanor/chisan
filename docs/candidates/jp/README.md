# Japan discovery methods

Apply the [shared candidate workflow](../README.md) and the
[Japan rules and source ceilings](../../../data/csv/jp/AGENTS.md). Record each
prefecture's exact source, query, date and cutoff in its area note.

## Prefectural beverage guilds

The [national guild index](https://www.japansake.or.jp/sake/link/index.html)
locates prefectural associations. The brewery search commonly uses
`japansake.or.jp/sakagura/jp/<prefecture>/`; Kochi uses `kouchi`. SAKETIMES
at `jp.sake-times.com/sakagura/<prefecture>` can assist with reading and
cross-checking a roster.

Do not assume sake is the relevant product in every prefecture: Kagoshima and
Miyazaki also require shochu sources, Okinawa awamori, and Kumamoto the Kuma
shochu guild.

## Other national routes

- Member directories for 和菓子, 米菓 and 乾麺 can expose a member name, address
  and submitted website.
- The organic JAS register can locate certified operators and declared sites.
- Direct-sale markets and regional-product directories can lead to named
  makers, but collective or cooperative pages must be followed to the
  productive unit.
- When a source enforces request limits, record the resumption point in the area
  note rather than this README.

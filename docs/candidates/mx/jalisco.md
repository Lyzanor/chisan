# Jalisco — candidates

- Target CSV: `data/csv/mx/centro-occidente/jalisco.csv`
- Source: Consejo Regulador del Tequila, *Marcas de tequila de envasado de origen
  certificadas*, XLSX updated 3 August 2026, linked from
  https://www.crt.org.mx/en/brands-and-associates/
- Searched: 16 August 2026
- Batch scope: the companies whose registered address is an appellation town.
  The ones filed at a Guadalajara-metro address are a separate batch below,
  because that address is the office and the distillery is elsewhere.

Every entry below is a company the CRT certifies to bottle tequila inside the
appellation, with the brands the same file lists for it. What that proves is
identity and the appellation, not current activity and not online sales, so
each one still needs its own current source before it becomes a row.

Two columns of the source file must not be copied into the CSV. The address is
the fiscal one, and the phone appended to it is not a contact at all: across
companies with three or more brands the number increments by exactly one per
brand row. `municipio` and `telefono` both have to come from the producer.

`categoria` for all of them is `Destilados y licores`.

# United States discovery methods

Apply the [shared candidate workflow](../README.md) and the
[United States rules and source ceilings](../../../data/csv/us/AGENTS.md). Keep
state-specific queries, dates, cutoffs and unresolved candidates in the state
note.

## Reusable national routes

- Search the [USDA Local Food Directories](https://www.ams.usda.gov/services/local-regional/food-directories-listings)
  for named on-farm markets and single-farm CSAs, then verify the producer
  independently.
- Use Organic Integrity operation name, scope and address as identity and
  de-duplication signals before locating the current public producer.
- Match federal alcohol premises or labels to a public producer through address,
  domain, brand or contact.
- State agriculture departments, extension programs and commodity associations
  are often the most useful discovery route. Record the exact directory,
  covered producer type, filters and source ceiling because they differ by
  state.

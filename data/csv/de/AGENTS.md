# Germany — temporary source note

Keep this note only while `data/csv/de/**` lacks per-row evidence.

- The inherited catalog came from an OpenStreetMap farm-shop extract. A
  `Hofladen`, vending hut or `Milchtankstelle` is a sales point, not proof that
  the listed entity produces what it sells. Confirm qualifying activity first.
- The beer queues in `docs/candidates/de/**` also use OSM and Wikidata only for
  discovery. `craft=brewery` still needs verification; `industrial` needs plant
  and producer-identity review; `microbrewery` often identifies hospitality
  that merely serves beer.
- `municipio` is the Gemeinde, never the Ortsteil; keep the latter in
  `direccion`.
- Treat inherited category, contacts and `opening_hours` as unverified source
  assertions until a current producer source confirms them.

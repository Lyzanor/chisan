# Ireland

## Operating state
- Priorities: resolve held candidates, strengthen admitted rows with current
  producer sources and improve productive-unit location.

## Country rules
- `municipio` is the town or village the producer publishes, not a local
  authority or townland.
- Rural addresses often name a townland. Keep it in `direccion` and use a
  nearby town as `municipio` only when the address or another reliable source
  supports that relationship.
- An Eircode identifies one address. Its routing key does not establish the
  county or productive municipality.

## Source ceilings
- IrishCraftBeer.ie is a third-party brewery directory. Its county, URL and
  closure markers require independent confirmation and do not prove current
  activity or domain ownership.
- The EU protected-name register supports a protected product and its area, not
  the producers currently making it.
- Dairy and meat establishment registers support the approval, address and
  activity columns they publish. Review role and processing type carefully:
  traders, cold stores, slaughterhouses and non-processing purchasers are not
  automatically qualifying producers.
